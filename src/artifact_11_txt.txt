AEGIS-SOL OPERATIONAL RUNBOOK
================================================================================
INCIDENT STAGE          ACTION REQUIRED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Key Compromise       Trigger: Admin invokes `update_agent(new_pubkey)`.
                        Impact: Instantly revokes compromised agent key without 
                        draining TVL or pausing contract.
                        
2. Market Anomaly       Trigger: Autonomous Sentinel halts rebalances if 
                        slippage > 5% or velocity cap is reached.
                        SecOps: Inspect Discord/Resend alert payload for root cause.

3. Emergency Freeze     Trigger: Multisig Governance invokes `set_emergency_freeze(true)`.
                        Impact: Freezes deposits and automated rebalancing.
                        
4. Capital Recovery     Prerequisite: Vault must be frozen.
                        Action: Admin executes `emergency_withdraw()` directly to
                        designated institutional cold treasury address.
