    participant K as Sentinel Keeper
    participant V as Aegis Vault PDA
    participant R as On-Chain Risk Engine
    participant S as Solana Runtime
    participant W as Enterprise Webhook (Discord/Resend)

    K->>V: execute_rebalance(target_allocation, max_slippage_bps)

        V->>V: Set state = VaultStatus::Frozen

    else Parameters Within Risk Bounds
        R-->>V: Invariant OK
        V->>V: Update internal accounting & velocity window
        V->>S: Emit RebalanceExecutedEvent
        S-->>K: Transaction Confirmed (Signature)
        K->>W: POST Telemetry Audit (Severity: INFO)
    end
